What is Deforestation?

It refers to the large-scale removal of trees and forests, often to make way for agriculture, urban development, or logging. Deforestation has significant environmental impacts, including loss of biodiversity, disruption of ecosystems, and contribution to climate change due to increased carbon dioxide levels in the atmosphere.

Important Facts

  1. Approximately 18 million acres of forest are lost each year, equivalent to 27 soccer fields every minute.
  2. Deforestation contributes to about 15% of global greenhouse gas emissions, worsening climate change.
  3. Beef accounts for 40% of global deforestation, as more land is needed to nourish cows to keep up with global demand.
  4. Brazil and Indonesia account for almost half of tropical deforestation.
  5. Leading banks financed about $119 billion to companies linked to deforestation.
  6. Indigenous People protect about 80% of the world's remaining biodiversity.
