What is Climate Change?

Climate change... to be continued.

Important Facts

  1. 2024 was the warmest year since records began in 1850.
  2. Climate change is causing more frequent and severe weather events.
  3. Global sea levels have risen by about 8 inches since 1880.
  4. The Arctic is warming twice as fast as the rest of the planet.
  5. Carbon dioxide levels are at their highest in 3 million years.
  6. Renewable energy sources are growing rapidly, but fossil fuels still dominate.
